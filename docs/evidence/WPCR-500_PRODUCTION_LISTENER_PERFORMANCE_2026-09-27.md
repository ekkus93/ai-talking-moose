## Production-path scope

The acceptance binary's `--production-listener` path executes `run_production_listener_performance_acceptance`, which starts the production native listener thread around the real pinned sherpa KWS session and the authoritative capture/runtime composition. It is not the standalone KWS-session benchmark.

The production listener acceptance records, per platform:

- listener startup duration;
- idle CPU while capture is active and silence frames are routed;
- peak resident memory before/after listener startup and startup delta;
- mean and p95 native KWS inference latency;
- wake-to-command-ASR, pre-roll startup, command-start, and total activation timing;
- handoff sample count;
- repeated wake/command/resume cycles;
- repeated enable/disable cycles;
- peak/final native-session counts;
- final capture ownership and runtime phase;
- ring-buffer and handoff-pre-roll sample deltas.

The acceptance fails closed unless inference ran, handoff audio was delivered, peak native sessions stayed at one, final native sessions returned to zero, capture was released, final runtime was Disabled, and retained ring/handoff buffers returned to their baseline.
